Washington County Profile

With over 600,000 residents, Washington County is Oregon's second-most populous county. More than 260,000 people live outside a city, in rural or urban unincorporated areas, the most of any Oregon county. It's home to Intel, Genentech and Nike's world headquarters, plus nurseries, wineries and farmland.
